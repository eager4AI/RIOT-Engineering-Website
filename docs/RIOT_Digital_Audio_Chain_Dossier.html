Host and device record

  • Host: Debian desktop named cameraHTPC.

  • Audio stack: PipeWire 0.3.65 with WirePlumber.

  • DAC: Amanero Combo384, ALSA card Amanero, device 0.

  • CamillaDSP version recorded as 4.1.3.

  • CamillaGUI backend runs on localhost:5005.

  • UMIK-1 is the REW microphone and is recognised as U18db.

Configuration decisions

  • Use one CamillaDSP path for both MPD and browser playback.

  • EasyEffects was removed because it created a redundant processing path.

  • Keep the digital chain documented separately from the room and speaker records, but attach its exact configuration to every measurement that depends on source level or filtering.

Troubleshooting history

  • A DAC USB glitch was resolved during RIOT system development.

  • A left-channel hiss was previously suspected to originate in the DAC path.

  • PipeWire/WirePlumber can lose ALSA sinks if CamillaDSP and MPD start before the audio services. Recovery sequence: stop MPD and CamillaDSP, restart PipeWire, pipewire-pulse and WirePlumber, confirm the Loopback sink, then start CamillaDSP and MPD.

  • Relevant services: camilladsp.service, mpd.service and camillagui.service.

Level references

  • Earlier system work used approximately 2.5 Vrms DAC output as the reference, with approximately 1.9-2.0 Vrms after crossover filtering.

  • A later DAC/output investigation reported approximately 1.4 Vrms and remains one reason for scope-assisted tracing.

  • Every future measurement record must state the level at the source and the level after the crossover.

Where to resume

Export and archive the current CamillaDSP configuration, then use the new scope and generator to verify the source level at 100 Hz, 1 kHz and 10 kHz before diagnosing the JFET cascode or any amplifier.
