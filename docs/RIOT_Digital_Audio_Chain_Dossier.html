Host and device record

  • Host: Debian desktop named cameraHTPC.

  • Audio stack: PipeWire 0.3.65 with WirePlumber.

  • DAC: Amanero Combo384, ALSA card Amanero, device 0.

  • CamillaDSP version recorded as 4.1.3.

  • CamillaGUI backend runs on localhost:5005.

  • UMIK-1 is the REW microphone and is recognised as U18db.

Configuration decisions

  • Use one CamillaDSP path for both MPD and browser playback.

  • EasyEffects was removed because it created a redundant processing path.

  • Keep the digital chain documented separately from the room and speaker records, but attach its exact configuration to every measurement that depends on source level or filtering.

Troubleshooting history

  • A DAC USB glitch was resolved during RIOT system development.

  • A left-channel hiss was previously suspected to originate in the DAC path.

  • PipeWire/WirePlumber can lose ALSA sinks if CamillaDSP and MPD start before the audio services. Recovery sequence: stop MPD and CamillaDSP, restart PipeWire, pipewire-pulse and WirePlumber, confirm the Loopback sink, then start CamillaDSP and MPD.

  • Relevant services: camilladsp.service, mpd.service and camillagui.service.

Level references

  • Earlier system work used approximately 2.5 Vrms DAC output as the reference, with approximately 1.9-2.0 Vrms after crossover filtering.

  • A later DAC/output investigation reported approximately 1.4 Vrms and remains one reason for scope-assisted tracing.

  • Every future measurement record must state the level at the source and the level after the crossover.

Separate analog buffer-board test · 24 October 2026

Scope: This separate op-amp buffer-board test is reported in the connected chat DAC board voltage measurement (conversation 692a1011-6194-8331-a223-6c83f9056418). It is not the active DAC → 50 kΩ ALPS → Headbanger FE path and does not change the system baseline.

  • Owner-reported drawing interpretation: the signal reaches the op-amp through a 110 Ω series resistor. The user corrected an earlier interpretation that bypassed it. A second resistor was identified from owner-supplied colour bands as 45 kΩ, but the drawing attachment was unavailable and its node placement is not independently confirmed here.
  • Owner-reported wiring error and correction: the 100 Ω feedback link had first been strapped between pins 2 and 3 rather than pins 1 and 2. The owner corrected the link arrangement to pins 1/2 and 6/7 and reported that the earlier +14.5 V DC output condition resulted from the wrong connection. Exact board pin/net mapping requires the actual schematic or continuity trace.
  • Reported after correction: output DC offset was 12 mV left and 4 mV right. The owner also reported +14.5 V at pin 8 before adding the feedback links, then +13.2 to +13.5 V, with the negative rail changing from −14.8 V to −14.2 to −14.3 V. Test points, load, meter setup, supply conditions and photos are absent; these remain owner-reported, not a complete characterization.
  • A prior configuration reportedly produced 9.5 Vrms from a 2.0 Vrms input and was rejected as unsuitable for unity gain. The corrected configuration’s AC gain was not reported. The final “no changes” referred to the input network at that stage; it is not approval for further redesign.
  • The owner asked about adding a WIMA MKS4 input coupling capacitor and noted a marked “101” capacitor. The image was unavailable and the capacitor’s connection was not established. Do not assign its function from the marking alone.
