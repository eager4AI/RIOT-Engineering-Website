Evidence classes and provenance

VERIFIED: a directly inspected source/artifact establishes the stated fact, within its scope. MEASURED: an identifiable original source records a numerical measurement and conditions; it remains source-reported unless independently repeated. OWNER-CONFIRMED: a directly recovered owner statement. INFERRED: an interpretation or hypothesis, with its basis. UNVERIFIED: a reconstruction, requested lead or claim without recovered primary support.

A verified message is not a verified electrical circuit. A reported repair outcome is not a current bench signoff. Forum author spartaco02 has not been independently identified as the RIOT owner; forum reports remain attributed to that author.

F1 — trying to resurrect an Orion 2150GX after massive short. The following is a concise attributed recovery note, not a recreated schematic:

4 April 2025, spartaco02: shorted speaker terminals damaged the supply. Reported parts include 2N6488/2N6491, replacements BD911/BD912, SF163/SF163A, LM317T/LM337T and MCT2V. The text also says 2N5488 and MSPA06; retain these spelling conflicts. Thermal-protection disconnection did not resolve REM symptoms. Perry Babin attributed bulb-limited startup failure to the self-oscillating supply and suggested a fused test; this is historical advice, not a new test instruction.
Reported readings: initially +1.7/−2 V at rectifiers using driver-output ground; REM switched 12/0 V. Post #4: with bulb removed and 15 A fuse, rectifier pin 2 measured +39.7/−39.7 V REM-on and +0.9/−0.9 V off; approximately ten minutes without reported heating. Posts #9/#15: paired Zeners marked IN4751A735 / 1N4751A, stated 30 V; measured 26.3 V on, 0.6 V off. Probe red cathode, black anode.
Perry identified regulation of the output-transistor supply; the author initially distinguished rectified main rails from driver winding. A supplied 280GX drawing was described as similar, not identical. The accessible extract ends at post #17, before restoration confirmation.

The archived readings describe troubleshooting conditions. They do not establish the accepted repaired configuration. The approximate main/auxiliary rail architecture requested for recovery is recorded separately as UNVERIFIED, not silently substituted for those readings.
