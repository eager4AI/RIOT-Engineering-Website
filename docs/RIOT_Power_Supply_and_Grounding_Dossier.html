Power Supply and Grounding Record

Update 24 October 2026 · Session evidence for the RIOT signal and power chassis

Evidence status

OWNER-CONFIRMED identifies the owner’s description of the construction and wiring. MEASURED identifies values reported from a physical measurement. Interpretations and limits are kept separate. This record is not a safety certification.

Signal chassis and audio reference

  • OWNER-CONFIRMED: wooden signal chassis.
  • OWNER-CONFIRMED: Amanero-based DAC outputs connect to a 50 kΩ ALPS stereo potentiometer through solid-core CAT5 wiring. Left, right, and common 0 V are carried to the pot.
  • OWNER-CONFIRMED: the DAC feeds the Headbanger FE through the ALPS; it does not feed the FE directly. The pot is a passive attenuator, not a galvanic isolator or active buffer.
  • OWNER-CONFIRMED: DAC signal 0 V, ALPS 0 V, Headbanger FE signal 0 V, FE supply 0 V, and associated circuit returns are joined at the signal star-ground.

Power chassis architecture

OWNER-CONFIRMED The separate wooden power chassis contains a three-conductor mains connection, mains EMI filter, two R-core transformers, and two CRC-filtered DC supplies. Three-conductor DC umbilicals carry positive rail, 0 V center tap, and negative rail to the signal chassis. Supply center taps / 0 V references connect into the grounding arrangement.

The owner confirms an installed resistor/capacitor ground-lift network between circuit 0 V and mains protective earth. Historical documentation reportedly gives a nominal 10 Ω resistor. The exact resistor value, capacitor specification, and full network wiring remain UNVERIFIED pending reconciliation with original build records and schematics.

No output-noise voltage, hum spectrum, or signal-to-noise ratio was measured in this session. This remains a subjective operational observation.

Scope-grounding gate

The earlier earth-referenced working assumption is superseded by the 27 October USB-power correction below; actual scope-common-to-earth paths are not established. Connecting a probe ground to the signal star may add a protective-earth path that bypasses some or all of the existing ground-lift impedance. USB/computer paths must also be considered.

  • Preserve required protective earth on equipment that has it; do not infer that the reported two-pin scope adapter includes PE.
  • Do not attach a grounded probe clip to an arbitrary supply rail or across a floating circuit element.
  • Do not treat the 11.3 Ω result as a complete live-measurement safety assessment.
  • The actual scope-to-DAC connection has not been performed.

27 October 2026 — USB power and isolation correction

OWNER-REPORTED: the DOS1102S is powered by a two-pin 5 V USB wall adapter with no protective-earth conductor through that adapter. Earlier archive instructions to treat clips as earth-referenced were a conservative working assumption, not a measured property of this instrument. The actual BNC-common/USB/PE paths remain unverified.

The owner first reported a 220:220 / 230:230 V transformer recommendation for the oscilloscope, then revised their understanding to powering the device under test. The adviser did not know the scope model or its USB supply. This is a corrected interpretation, not a confirmed installation or proof of the adviser’s exact intent.

Engineering procedure: a genuine separate-winding isolation transformer can isolate a DUT supply from mains conductors where appropriate; it is not an automatic accuracy upgrade or guaranteed mains-noise filter. It does not create isolated scope channels or make exposed mains safe. Preserve all required protective-earth bonds. Never float a conventional earthed scope by defeating its earth. A two-pin adapter does not establish rated floating-input safety. Treat channel commons as interconnected unless model-specific evidence establishes otherwise; a computer or other connected equipment can introduce an earth path.

PENDING: map actual scope/adapter/USB and DUT earth paths, confirm instrument/probe ratings and as-built ground-lift wiring, then select the measurement reference. A suitably rated differential/isolated measurement system is required for floating or hazardous nodes. CH1−CH2 math does not isolate ground clips or increase input ratings. The existing 11.3 Ω result is unchanged and is not safety certification; no new DAC connection, scope noise test or physical isolation validation is reported.
