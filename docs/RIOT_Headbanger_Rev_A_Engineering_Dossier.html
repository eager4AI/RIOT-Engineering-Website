Mono Class A headphone amplifier and preamplifier

This dossier records the current Rev A design, the evidence supplied with the KiCad engineering-review package, the remaining release blockers and the exact sequence for prototype bring-up. The board is routed and electrically checked, but it is not yet released for fabrication or headphone use.

Architecture

  • J5 unbalanced input.

  • U310 and LSK170 all-JFET cascoded source follower.

  • C49/R38 AC coupling into the complementary EF2 stage.

  • KSC1845 and KSA992 drivers, BD139 VBE multiplier, KSC2690A/KSA1220A output pair.

  • Two series 4700 uF output capacitors with DC midpoint bias.

  • DC and rail-fault sensing with delayed 24 V Omron relay output.

  • On-board 18-0-18 VAC rectification, CRC filtering and LM317/LM337 +/-15 V regulation.

Set and verify current by measuring TP2-TP1 across R35, where 14.5 mV corresponds to 1.45 mA and 23.0 mV corresponds to 2.30 mA. These are generic-model calculations, not a substitute for measurements on the purchased devices.

Thermal and mechanical requirements

  • Q3, Q5 and Q4 require close thermal contact on one shared, electrically insulated heatsink rated at 5 K/W or lower.

  • Q3 and Q4 tabs must be electrically isolated from the heatsink and from one another as required by their node potentials.

  • U3 and U4 require separate heatsinks; their tabs are at different electrical potentials.

  • The enclosure still requires a safe mains inlet, primary fuse, switch, protective earth and transformer mounting.

  • The circuit has no output short-circuit or over-current limiter. Do not hot-plug a connector that can short the output.

Bring-up procedure

  1. Verify the actual lead order and authenticity of U310, LSK170, KSC1845, KSA992, KSC2690A, KSA1220A and BD139.

  2. Populate rectifiers, CRC network and regulators only. Use fused primary wiring and a current-limited AC source.

  3. Set the regulators to +15.00 V and -15.00 V. Accept +/-0.10 V and less than 100 mV rail imbalance.

  4. Load each rail at 120 mA for 30 minutes. Confirm regulation, temperature and absence of thermal shutdown.

  5. Populate the JFET front end. Set the selected current and log source, drain, cascode-drain and BUF_OUT voltages.

  6. Populate the EF2 without K1 or headphones. Start from a current-limited +/-5 V bench supply and increase in steps.

  7. Set 35.25 mV across R11 and R12. The readings must agree within 5 mV.

  8. Thermal-soak for 60 minutes at +/-15 V. Confirm 65-85 mA settled idle current, AMP_OUT within +/-100 mV and cases below 80 C.

  9. Confirm CAPMID is -9.5 V to -11.5 V and J1 is below 10 mV DC after relay closure.

  10. Test relay timing and DC protection with a dummy load, including +/-1 V forced faults and loss of either rail.

  11. Use a 16 ohm, 5 W non-inductive dummy load and oscilloscope before any headphone connection.

  12. Use a disposable 32 ohm headphone pair for the first listening test; the 15/16 ohm Ananda comes last.

Known risks and unresolved questions

  • Real-device pinouts, authenticity and semiconductor spread are not yet verified.

  • Generic transistor models do not establish distortion, stability or safe thermal behaviour.

  • Oscilloscope stability with a 15/16 ohm load and reactive headphone cable has not been tested.

  • Relay timing, trip threshold and rail-loss behaviour remain simulation-only until measured on the assembled board.

  • Shared output-heatsink mechanics and electrical insulation remain enclosure-level work.

  • The 115 parity warnings are classified as non-electrical, but the classification should be retained with the package.

Where to resume

Do not reroute Rev A unless a genuine defect is found. The next engineering action is controlled prototype bring-up, beginning with regulator-only testing and real-device pinout verification. Append measured values to this dossier after each gate; do not rely on memory or chat history.

Signal-chain interface evidence · 24 October 2026

OWNER-CONFIRMED: the Amanero DAC output is routed over solid-core CAT5 wiring to a 50 kΩ ALPS stereo potentiometer, then to the Headbanger FE. The DAC does not feed the FE directly. The ALPS is a passive attenuator, not a buffer or galvanic isolator. DAC signal 0 V, ALPS 0 V, FE signal 0 V, FE power-supply 0 V, and associated returns share the signal-chassis star-ground.

This records the system interface described by the owner; it does not establish the internal FE input wiring, loaded transfer response, noise voltage, or a completed scope connection. The signal chassis is owner-confirmed as wooden. Ground topology, the 11.3 Ω measurement, and live-probing safety limits are recorded in the power and grounding dossier. The DOS1102S basic channel check passed, but the actual scope-to-DAC connection and DAC analog measurement remain outstanding. See the DAC investigation and bench evidence register.
