Measured commissioning record · 24 October 2026

Basic functional check completed

Both channels were checked using the instrument's internal generator and a direct BNC-to-BNC cable with a nominal 1 kHz sine. The cable contains no 10× probe: set channel attenuation to 1×. A prior 10× channel setting caused approximately tenfold amplitude over-reporting.

CH1 extrema were reported as approximately +504 mV and −520 mV. Stable triggering and a clean trace without visible clipping were obtained after trigger, vertical-scale, and acquisition adjustments. Both channels passed this basic functional check; this is not formal calibration and does not establish bandwidth accuracy, noise floor, or distortion performance. No screenshot or raw capture was attached.

Match attenuation to the connection

  • Direct BNC cable: channel attenuation 1×.
  • Physical 10× probe: channel attenuation 10×, with the probe switch and scope setting matched.
  • Before evaluating clipping, make waveform peaks visible and confirm automatic frequency and peak-to-peak readings against the trace.

Grounding gate: Treat the scope's probe ground clips as earth-referenced. The RIOT signal star is connected through an owner-confirmed resistor/capacitor ground-lift network to protective earth; a scope ground may create another path. The 24 October 11.3 Ω resistance reading is not a complete safety validation. Do not make a live DAC connection until the scope-grounding and USB/computer paths have been assessed. Do not disconnect scope protective earth.
