Owner's research brief

Investigate a derived Class AB “muscle amp” using a BJT current stage inspired by the Orion GX2150. The earlier conversation calls the unit “GX” and links a repair discussion using the name “2150GX”; the exact model designation remains to be reconciled with the actual unit.

The owner's stated aim is to understand how to translate the car-amplifier concept to a home amplifier, rather than replicate the PAM directly in the home “GC”. PAM and GC are retained as the owner's terms; their exact expansion and intended circuit boundaries are not established by the recovered excerpt.

This is a separate speculative branch. It does not revise the accepted Headbanger, LM3886, Aleph Mini, Ciuffoli, crossover or loudspeaker configurations.
